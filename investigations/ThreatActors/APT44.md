# APT44
APT44—widely known as Sandworm—is a destructive Russian state-sponsored cyber group linked to military intelligence
APT44 is responsible for some of the most damaging cyberattacks in history: Ukraine Power Grid : Caused blackouts by attacking electrical substations.NotPetya (2017): Released destructive wiper malware that caused billions of dollars in global damage. Olympic Destroyer (2018): Disrupted systems at the Pyeongchang Winter Olympics.
